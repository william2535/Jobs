The two toolbox calculator modules, original pack manifest, pack.json and six
original picture guides are unchanged. The diagnostic-calculators dependency is
an exact extract containing only the original warning, blank/r/unitSets helpers
and parseDiagnosticQuantity. It includes no FINDR field engine/model, UI,

These tools perform arithmetic on entered values. They do not perform tests,
authorise live work, infer equipment identity, approve system design or certify
compliance. The original tool-specific assumptions, limitations and sources are
included with the calculators and guides. External source material remains with
its publisher and is not redistributed.

FINDR Resistor Lab and FESS revision components for Jobs

Source: https://github.com/william2535/Findr
Source revision: 8bdd8b03feb8babd6266b80b57dbc5d53efeadb2
Original location: public/app/

Reused at the project owner's explicit request. Original source comments,
question explanations, attribution and external reference links are retained.
The inspected source does not include an overarching licence for these
components. This integration does not grant an MIT licence or otherwise
relicense the FINDR source, educational question content or cited material.
Their existing rights remain with their respective owners.

The resistor, quiz and full-paper engines, generated-audio module, paper
view and question data are byte-for-byte copies. Controllers adapt storage,
local navigation and lifecycle handling; styles use Jobs' light blue theme.
Exact original and included SHA-256 hashes are in vendor/findr-extras/PROVENANCE.json in the editable Jobs source.

FESS quizzes and papers are practice only, not official examination papers
or assessment results. The included full papers have a 60-minute practice
timer. The original notice that Highfield's cited FESS kit v5.0 lists
60 questions in 75 minutes is retained, with its source link.

No tool calculation, practice answer or score writes to engineer-confirmed
job results. Saved tool progress uses a separate local Jobs database.
